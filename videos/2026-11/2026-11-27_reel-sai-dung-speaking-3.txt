✅ Đáp án 4 câu:
1. I prefer reading to watching TV. (prefer A to B)
2. It's the first time I've visited Hanoi. (the first time + hiện tại hoàn thành)
3. I'd rather stay at home tonight. (would rather + V nguyên mẫu)
4. People spend a lot of time using smartphones. (spend time + V-ing)
