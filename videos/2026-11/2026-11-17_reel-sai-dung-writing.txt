BÌNH LUẬN ĐẦU TIÊN (dán sau khi Reel lên):
📌 Đáp án 4 câu:
1) ❌ The amount of people using buses has risen.
✅ The number of people using buses has risen. (people đếm được → the number of)
2) ❌ Since 2010, the figure rose steadily.
✅ Since 2010, the figure has risen steadily. (Since + mốc → hiện tại hoàn thành)
3) ❌ Many cities are overcrowded, this leads to traffic jams.
✅ Many cities are overcrowded, which leads to traffic jams. (tránh comma splice)
4) ❌ Studying abroad has benefits, such as students become independent.
✅ Studying abroad has benefits, such as greater independence. (such as + cụm danh từ)

✍️ Thử thách: Câu này sai ở đâu? "The amount of tourists visiting Hoi An has increased since 2015, this is good for the economy." Comment câu sửa nhé!
