BÌNH LUẬN ĐẦU TIÊN (dán sau khi Reel lên):
📌 3 câu nâng band – chủ đề Urbanisation:
1) Many people move to cities because they want to find jobs.
→ Rural residents are migrating to cities in search of better employment prospects.
2) Cities are growing fast, so house prices are very high.
→ Rapid urban growth has put decent housing beyond the reach of many families.
3) People who live in big cities often feel stressed and lonely.
→ Those living in large cities often experience high levels of stress and isolation.

✍️ Thử thách: Viết lại câu "Cities have many cars, so the air is dirty." bằng cụm danh từ (nominalisation). Comment câu của bạn nhé!
