BÌNH LUẬN ĐẦU TIÊN (dán sau khi Reel lên):
📌 3 câu paraphrase trong video:
1. Some people think governments should spend more money on public health.
→ Some contend that governments should allocate more funding to public health.
2. Fast food is becoming more and more popular among teenagers.
→ Fast food has been gaining popularity among adolescents.
3. Many people do not do enough exercise, and this causes health problems.
→ Insufficient physical activity leads to health problems for many people.

🔥 Thử thách: Paraphrase câu "Many people think that doctors should be paid more." Comment bài của bạn, thầy góp ý nhé!
