BÌNH LUẬN ĐẦU TIÊN (dán sau khi Reel lên):
📌 3 câu paraphrase trong video:
1. Many people believe that university education should be free.
→ It is widely argued that tertiary education ought to be provided free of charge.
2. The number of cars on the road is increasing.
→ There has been an increase in the number of vehicles on the roads.
3. Some people think children should learn a foreign language at primary school.
→ Some argue that foreign languages should be taught to children at primary level.
✍️ Thử thách: paraphrase câu đề "Many young people spend too much time on social media." Comment câu của bạn nhé!
