BÌNH LUẬN ĐẦU TIÊN (dán sau khi Reel lên):
📌 3 câu nâng cấp – Speaking Part 1 (Teachers):
1) Yes. My teacher is very kind.
→ Yes, I do. My primary teacher was very kind and patient with us.
2) I like teacher who funny.
→ I prefer teachers who make lessons fun and easy to follow.
3) In Vietnam, we have Teacher Day, we give flowers.
→ On Teachers' Day, students usually give their teachers flowers to say thank you.

✍️ Thử thách: Trả lời câu "Who was your favourite teacher at school?" trong 2 câu. Comment nhé!
